# EU Legislation & Case Law

Access EU law directly from Claude Code.

## Features

- **Search** EU legislation and case law by topic (24 languages)
- **Retrieve** full text of Directives, Regulations, Decisions, Treaties, CJEU judgments
- **Check status** — in force, amended, repealed
- **View modifications** — what acts amended this one
- **Transposition status** — which Member States transposed a directive
- **Procedure history** — legislative procedure from proposal to adoption

## Usage

```
"Give me the GDPR"
"Has the Whistleblower Directive been transposed in France?"
"What modifications have been made to CSRD?"
"Show me the procedure history of the AI Act"
"Find CJEU judgments on cookie consent"
```
