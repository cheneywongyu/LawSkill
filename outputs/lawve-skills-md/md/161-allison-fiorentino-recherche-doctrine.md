## Original README

Academic research across French, European, and international legal scholarship databases. Use this skill when the user requests searches for doctrinal articles, dissertations, academic books, or university legal publications — including comparative law. Primary sources: ISIDORE (CNRS social sciences aggregator including Cairn.info, Persée, OpenEdition), HAL (French open archive), OpenAlex (global open database of 250M+ works), Semantic Scholar, CrossRef, Persée (direct OAI), and CORE. Covers both francophone AND international legal scholarship with a built-in bilingual workflow for comparative law. Also triggers for: annotated bibliography, state of the art, legal literature review, author search, citation verification, bibliometric analysis.
